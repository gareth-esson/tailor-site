Staff in special schools, alternative provision and SEMH settings teach
RSE to pupils who need it adapted to how they communicate and learn. We
teach RSE directly in these settings through
[Circuits](/services/rse-for-send-and-ap/), our workbook-led programme for
SEND and AP, and we've trained staff, written policy and run workshops in
special schools and alternative provision, including SEMH settings. This
session brings that work to your staff.

It draws on our guides to [RSE for pupils with
SEND](/blog/rse-send-practical-approaches/) and [developing RSE in special
schools](/blog/complete-guide-rse-special-schools/).
