This is the foundation session, for staff new to RSE and for teams who
want a shared approach to it. It covers how to set up RSE lessons safely,
how to pitch content for each age group, and how to respond to questions
and disclosures.

The training is aligned to the DfE statutory guidance that has applied
since September 2026, KCSIE and the PSHE Association framework. Staff
practise answers to questions pupils have handed in anonymously, so the
framework is tested on real examples during the session.
