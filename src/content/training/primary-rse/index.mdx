Primary RSE has its own content and its own questions. The 2026 statutory
guidance makes correct names for body parts statutory content and sets
firmer expectations about what parents get to see. This session covers
both, along with puberty, personal safety and the foundations of consent,
pitched for younger children.

It draws on our articles on [what the 2026 guidance changes for primary
RSE](/blog/2026-rse-guidance-primary-schools/) and [teaching puberty before
it happens](/blog/teaching-puberty-primary-schools-guide/).
