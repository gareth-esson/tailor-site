Consent is statutory content, and the legal definition is where teaching
it starts. This session covers what the definition leaves open: pressure
and persistence, alcohol, age gaps, status, and consent for images and
messages.

One distinction runs through it: the difference between what is legal,
what is consensual, what is healthy and what is ethical. It builds on our
article [How to teach consent beyond "no means
no"](/blog/teaching-consent-beyond-no-means-no/).
