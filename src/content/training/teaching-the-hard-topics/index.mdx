Some RSE questions need more than subject knowledge: a question about
rape, a statement repeated from porn, a question that may be about the
pupil's own life. This session works through questions like these one at
a time, using questions pupils have handed in anonymously.

For each one, staff work out what they're trying to achieve, what they can
answer, what needs correcting, and when a question becomes a safeguarding
matter. The approach builds on our article [Teaching the hard topics when
you're not a specialist](/blog/teaching-hard-topics-when-not-a-specialist/),
which starts by working out what kind of hard a topic is.
