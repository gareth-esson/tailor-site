Misogyny, positive ideas of masculinity and the pull of online influencers
are named in the statutory guidance that has applied since September 2026.

The training uses the approach behind our [sample Year 9
sequence](/for-schools/teaching-about-misogyny-and-masculinity/): this
material reaches boys by attaching itself to things that are reasonable to
want, and a lesson that separates the need from the answer can challenge
the answer while keeping the boy in the conversation. Staff practise
responding to misogynistic comments with that distinction in mind.

The evidence comes from the
[What do boys actually think?](/resources/what-do-boys-actually-think/)
quiz, which sets out sixteen survey findings on what boys report and
believe, each sourced to a named survey.
