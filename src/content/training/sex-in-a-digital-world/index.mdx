The 2026 statutory guidance requires schools to teach about pornography,
and its secondary online-safety content covers sharing images and
deepfakes. This session teaches them as one subject: what porn shows
about sex and consent, the pressure to share images, how sextortion
works, and what the law says.

It draws on our articles on [teaching porn
literacy](/blog/teaching-porn-literacy-what-it-is-why-it-matters/) and on
[sharing nudes, sextortion and
deepfakes](/blog/sharing-nudes-sextortion-deepfakes-schools-2026/).
